💡 Monitoring Flow Information

This flowchart shows how LifeDot continuously monitors your heart rate in real-time. The system checks your BPM every 3 seconds and automatically triggers emergency alerts when critical values are detected (below 40 or above 150 BPM). Warning messages are shown for borderline values, keeping you informed about your heart health 24/7.
